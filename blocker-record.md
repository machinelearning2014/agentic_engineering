# Blocker Record Template

> Source: *Agentic Engineering* (ed. 2.0), Chapter 18.4 + Section 11.6.
> A route has stalled — the goal has NOT. Record the blocker in this form, then
> pivot to a materially different route. Four fields, always.

## Goal

_The objective that remains unmet._

<Write it here.>

## Route attempted

_The approach, decomposition, or technique just tried._

<Write it here.>

## Observed failure

_The exact error or the exact unmet criterion, quoted._

<Write it verbatim, not paraphrased.>

## Next route planned

_The materially different approach to try next, or “escalate to human” with reason._

<Write it here. A retry is valid only if it differs in kind — a different
decomposition, library, technique, or order of work.>
